# Try. Fail. Codify. Repeat.

**300 Days of AI as an Engineering Manager**

[Download slides (PDF)](./static/300-days-of-ai-em/slides.pdf){:target="_blank"}

Talk given in Polish at [Data Science Summit 2026](https://main.dssconf.pl/){:target="_blank"}. It is the updated version of [100 Days of AI as an Engineering Manager](./100-days-of-ai-em.md).

Most AI talks show code generation. I tried AI on the strategic and operational side of engineering management: not agentic coding, but running the work around it. This talk covers 300 days of that and what I learned along the way.

I used AI for managing people, delivery and execution, and for navigating the organization: delegation and ownership, roadmap planning, delivery syncs, team capacity, knowledge sharing, career development, stakeholder relationships, hiring and performance reviews. Some of it worked surprisingly well. A lot of it failed. Over time, three layers built up: behavioral guardrails, organizational memory and reusable skills. I share which tasks I delegate to AI, which need oversight and which I still do myself, and what the same loop means for teams moving toward the software factory.
